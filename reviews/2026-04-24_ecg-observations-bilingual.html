§ 01 — 波形解剖

P-QRS-T 複合波

Anatomy of the Waveform

所有的心電圖判讀,都是這個「基本波形」的放大、變形或缺失。熟悉正常波形,異常自然顯眼。

Every ECG observation is a deviation from — or an exaggeration of — this basic shape. Before reading abnormalities, know the normal.

各波 = 一次電氣事件

Each wave = one electrical event
P
心房去極化 — 心房收縮的電氣訊號Atrial depolarization (contraction)
QRS
心室去極化 — 正常寬度 ≤ 120 毫秒Ventricular depolarization. Normal ≤ 0.12 s
T
心室再極化 — 恢復靜息狀態Ventricular repolarization (recovery)
U
T 波後的小波 — 常見於低血鉀Small wave after T; prominent in hypokalemia
PR
房室傳導延遲 — 正常 120-200 msAV node delay, 0.12-0.20 s
ST
QRS 與 T 間的平直段 — 應位於基線Flat segment between QRS and T, on baseline
QT
心室去極化 + 再極化總時間 — 校正後為 QTcTotal ventricular depol + repol. Corrected = QTc
紙速 25 mm/s:一小格 = 40 ms,一大格 = 200 ms。振幅 10 mm/mV:一小格 = 0.1 mV。 Paper speed 25 mm/s — 1 small box = 40 ms, 1 large box = 200 ms. Voltage 10 mm/mV — 1 small box = 0.1 mV.
§ 02 — 心率

心率判讀

Heart Rate — Fast, Slow, or Just Right

靜息正常心率為每分鐘 60-100 下。低於 60 為心搏過緩;高於 100 為心搏過速。運動員常見靜息心搏過緩(40-50 下),屬於正常生理適應。

Normal resting rate is 60-100 bpm. Below 60 is bradycardia; above 100 is tachycardia. Athletes commonly show resting bradycardia as a normal adaptation.

NORMAL · 75 BPM R-R ~ 800 ms
正常 / Normal

正常竇性心率

Normal Sinus Rate
波形特徵:R-R 間距規則,每個間距約 3-5 大格,每分鐘 60-100 下。
What you see: Regular R-R intervals, each ~ 3-5 large boxes. Rate 60-100.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
竇房結以正常速率發動心搏,無需處置。
The heart is firing at rest from the sinus node at a healthy pace. No action needed.
BRADYCARDIA · 45 BPM R-R > 1000 ms
心搏過緩 / Bradycardia

心搏過緩 — 心率 < 60

Bradycardia — rate < 60
波形特徵:R-R 間距寬於 5 大格,節律本身常為正常竇性。
R-R intervals wider than 5 large boxes. Rhythm may itself be normal sinus.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
常見於運動員、睡眠中、使用 β 阻斷劑時;若伴隨頭暈、昏厥或房室傳導阻滯(見 §06),則需評估。
Common in athletes, during sleep, with beta-blockers. Concerning if symptomatic (dizziness, syncope) or with a block.
TACHYCARDIA · 130 BPM R-R < 500 ms
心搏過速 / Tachycardia

心搏過速 — 心率 > 100

Tachycardia — rate > 100
波形特徵:R-R 間距小於 3 大格;心率極快時 P 波可能融入前一個 T 波。
R-R intervals narrower than 3 large boxes. P waves may compress into preceding T waves.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
需視情境:焦慮、發燒、脫水、咖啡因所致的竇性心搏過速屬良性;持續性窄 QRS 心搏過速(SVT)或寬 QRS 心搏過速(VT)性質截然不同,見 §03。
Context matters: sinus tachycardia from anxiety, fever, dehydration, caffeine is benign. Sustained SVT or VT is a different matter.
§ 03 — 節律

節律觀察

Rhythm Observations

節律要回答兩件事:誰在發號施令?節拍規則嗎?竇性節律(竇房結發動、每個 QRS 前有 P 波)為正常。其餘皆為心律不整。

Rhythm asks: who is pacing the heart, and is the pacing regular? Sinus rhythm is the default. Anything else is an arrhythmia.

SINUS RHYTHM P→QRS 1:1
正常 / Normal

正常竇性節律 (NSR)

Normal Sinus Rhythm
判讀條件:節律規則、心率 60-100、導程 II 的 P 波向上、每個 P 後接一個 QRS。
Regular rhythm, rate 60-100, upright P in lead II, each P followed by QRS.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
預期中的正常節律。
The expected, healthy pattern.
SINUS ARRHYTHMIA R-R varies w/ breath
正常變異 / Normal variant

竇性心律不整 (呼吸性)

Sinus Arrhythmia
波形特徵:竇性節律但 R-R 間距隨呼吸週期性變化 — 吸氣加快、吐氣減慢。
Sinus rhythm with R-R intervals that speed up with inspiration and slow with expiration.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
自律神經健全的表現,尤其是年輕人。反映良好的 HRV(心率變異)。
A marker of healthy autonomic tone, especially in young people. Reflects good heart-rate variability.
ATRIAL FIBRILLATION No P waves · R-R irregular
心律不整 / Arrhythmia

心房顫動 (AFib)

Atrial Fibrillation
波形特徵:P 波消失,取而代之的是雜亂波動的基線;R-R 間距「不規則的不規則」,毫無模式可言。
No discernible P waves — chaotic wavy baseline. R-R "irregularly irregular".
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
心房顫抖而非收縮,血流滯留於左心耳易形成血栓,中風風險上升。常見於高齡、高血壓、過度飲酒、睡眠呼吸中止。通常需心臟超音波與 CHA₂DS₂-VASc 中風風險評估。
Atria quiver instead of contracting. Stroke risk rises from stagnant blood clotting in LAA. Evaluation: echocardiogram + CHA₂DS₂-VASc score.
ATRIAL FLUTTER Sawtooth · 4:1 conduction
心律不整 / Arrhythmia

心房撲動

Atrial Flutter
波形特徵:在 II、III、aVF 導程可見典型「鋸齒狀」撲動波(F 波)約每分鐘 300 次,心室以固定比例下傳(常見 2:1 或 4:1)。
Classic "sawtooth" F-waves at ~300/min with ratio conduction (often 2:1 or 4:1).
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
右心房內的折返迴路。中風風險與 AFib 相當;因解剖位置固定,常以導管燒灼術治療。
Re-entrant circuit in right atrium. Stroke risk similar to AFib. Often treated with catheter ablation.
PVC Wide · no P · compensatory pause
異位 / Ectopy

心室早期收縮 (PVCs)

Premature Ventricular Contractions
波形特徵:提早出現的寬大怪異 QRS,前方無 P 波,其後常有代償性停頓。
Early, wide, bizarre-looking QRS with no preceding P wave, usually followed by a compensatory pause.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
偶發 PVC 在結構正常的心臟極為常見且良性。但若每日 PVC 負擔 >10% 或運動時出現且有症狀,需進一步心臟檢查。
Occasional PVCs benign in structurally normal hearts. Frequent (>10%/day) or exercise-induced symptomatic PVCs warrant workup.
PAC Early narrow · abnormal P'
異位 / Ectopy

心房早期收縮 (PACs)

Premature Atrial Contractions
波形特徵:提早出現的窄 QRS,前方有形態異常的 P' 波(與正常竇性 P 波不同)。
Early narrow QRS preceded by an abnormally-shaped P' wave.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
極為常見且大多良性 — 咖啡因、壓力、酒精、疲勞皆可誘發。頻繁 PAC 可能是日後 AFib 的前兆。
Very common and usually benign — triggered by caffeine, stress, alcohol, fatigue. Frequent PACs may predispose to AFib.
VENTRICULAR TACHYCARDIA Wide QRS · regular · fast
急症 / Medical emergency

心室頻脈 (VT)

Ventricular Tachycardia
波形特徵:連續三個以上寬大怪異的 QRS(>120 ms),心率 >100。P 波消失、分離或逆行。
Three or more consecutive wide (>120 ms) QRS complexes at >100 bpm. P waves absent, dissociated, or retrograde.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning — 急症
可能危及生命。持續性 VT(>30 秒)或合併血流動力學不穩定需立即電擊整流/去顫。健檢中偶發非持續性 VT 罕見,但需立即轉介心臟科。
Potentially life-threatening. Sustained VT (>30 s) or hemodynamic instability requires immediate cardioversion/defibrillation.
§ 04 — 心電軸

心電軸

Electrical Axis

心室去極化在額狀面上的平均方向。正常範圍為 -30° 至 +90°。偏移可能提示腔室大小、傳導異常或潛在的心肺疾病。

The mean direction of ventricular depolarization in the frontal plane. Normal is between -30° and +90°.

AXIS DIAGRAM Frontal plane
參考 / Reference

心電軸判讀

Normal Axis & Deviations
快速判讀:若導程 I 與導程 II 的 QRS 皆為正向,則心電軸正常。
Quick rule: If QRS is upright in both lead I and lead II, the axis is normal.
左偏軸 (LAD) / Left Axis Deviation
導程 I 正向、導程 II 負向。常見原因:左前支傳導阻滯、左心室肥大、下壁心肌梗塞、高血鉀。
I positive, II negative. Causes: LAFB, LVH, inferior MI, hyperkalemia.
右偏軸 (RAD) / Right Axis Deviation
導程 I 負向、導程 II 正向。常見原因:右心室肥大、肺栓塞、慢性阻塞性肺病、側壁心梗、左後支傳導阻滯。
I negative, II positive. Causes: RVH, PE, COPD, lateral MI, LPFB.
§ 05 — 間期測量

間期測量

Interval Measurements

PR、QRS、QT(校正後為 QTc)是報告上三個關鍵的時間測量,以毫秒為單位。各自反映不同層次的傳導狀況。

PR, QRS, and QT (corrected as QTc) are the three key time measurements your ECG report will list.

LONG QT QTc > 480 ms
異常 / Abnormal

QT 延長

Prolonged QT / QTc
波形特徵:Q 波起點到 T 波終點的距離變長。校正後 QTc(Bazett 公式)男性 > 450 ms、女性 > 460 ms 為延長;> 500 ms 屬高風險。
QTc > 450 ms (men) / > 460 ms (women) is prolonged; > 500 ms high-risk.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
易誘發尖端扭轉型心室頻脈(Torsades),可惡化為心室顫動。原因包括先天性長 QT 症候群、低血鉀、低血鎂、低血鈣,以及一長串藥物(某些抗生素、抗黴菌、抗精神病、止吐劑、美沙冬等)。
Predisposes to torsades de pointes → VF. Causes: congenital LQTS, hypo-K/Mg/Ca, many drugs (antibiotics, antifungals, antipsychotics, antiemetics, methadone).
SHORT QT QTc < 340 ms
異常 / Abnormal

QT 過短

Short QT
波形特徵:QTc < 340-360 ms,T 波常顯得高聳且緊貼 QRS。
QTc < 340-360 ms, T waves often appearing tall and crowded against QRS.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
比長 QT 罕見得多。原因:高血鈣、毛地黃效應、先天性短 QT 症候群(與心律不整及猝死相關)。
Much rarer than long QT. Causes: hypercalcemia, digoxin effect, congenital SQTS (associated with arrhythmia and sudden death).
§ 06 — 傳導阻滯

電路上的障礙

Blocks in the Wiring

電氣訊號從心房 → 房室結 → 希氏束 → 左右支分。任一環節阻滯,皆會在心電圖上留下特定指紋。

The signal travels: atrium → AV node → bundle of His → bundle branches. A block anywhere shows up as a specific pattern.

1° AV BLOCK PR prolonged · all beats conduct
一度 / 1st-degree

一度房室傳導阻滯

First-Degree AV Block
波形特徵:每個 P 後皆有 QRS,但 PR 間期固定 > 200 ms。
Every P followed by QRS, but PR interval consistently > 200 ms.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
大多良性,常見於老化、運動員、迷走神經張力高者。除非極度延長(>300 ms)或有症狀,否則不需處置。
Often benign and common with age, athletic training, or vagal tone. Only significant if very prolonged or symptomatic.
2° MOBITZ I (Wenckebach) PR lengthens → drop
二度 / 2nd-degree

二度房室阻滯 Mobitz I 型 (文氏)

Mobitz Type I (Wenckebach)
波形特徵:PR 間期逐拍延長,直到某次 P 波無法下傳(QRS 脫落),然後重新開始循環。
PR interval lengthens progressively beat by beat until a P wave fails to conduct; cycle resets.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
阻滯位於房室結。多為良性,常見於運動員或迷走神經張力高者。甚少需要節律器。
Block at the AV node. Usually benign. Rarely needs pacing.
2° MOBITZ II PR constant · random drop
二度 / 2nd-degree

二度房室阻滯 Mobitz II 型

Mobitz Type II
波形特徵:PR 間期固定,但突然某次 P 波無預警地未下傳(QRS 脫落)。
PR interval stays constant, but a P wave suddenly fails to conduct without warning.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
阻滯位於房室結下方(希氏-浦金氏系統)。較 Mobitz I 危險,可能惡化為完全性房室阻滯。常需置入節律器。
Block below AV node (His-Purkinje). More dangerous — can progress to complete heart block. Pacemaker often indicated.
3° AV BLOCK AV dissociation
三度 / 3rd-degree

完全性房室傳導阻滯

Complete Heart Block
波形特徵:P 波以自己的節奏出現,QRS 以另一個較慢的節奏出現,兩者完全無關(房室分離)。
P waves march at their own rate. QRS marches independently. No relationship (AV dissociation).
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
心房與心室各自為政,心室依靠「逃逸節律」,慢而不可靠。必須置入節律器。
Atria and ventricles beat independently. Ventricles depend on escape pacemaker. Pacemaker required.
RBBB · V1 QRS > 120 ms · rsR' in V1
支分阻滯 / Bundle branch

右束支傳導阻滯 (RBBB)

Right Bundle Branch Block
波形特徵:QRS ≥ 120 ms;V1-V2 呈 rsR'(M 型);I、aVL、V5、V6 出現拖尾的 S 波。
QRS ≥ 120 ms, rsR' ("M-shape") in V1-V2, slurred S wave in I, aVL, V5, V6.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
常見且多為良性,尤其是老年人。可能與肺栓塞、慢阻肺、先天性心臟病(如心房中膈缺損)、右心室負荷有關。單獨存在時通常無需治療。
Often benign and common with age. Can be associated with PE, COPD, ASD, or RV strain.
LBBB · V6 QRS > 120 ms · broad R in V6
支分阻滯 / Bundle branch

左束支傳導阻滯 (LBBB)

Left Bundle Branch Block
波形特徵:QRS ≥ 120 ms;I、aVL、V5、V6 出現寬而切跡的 R 波;V1-V2 出現深 S;T 波方向常與 QRS 相反。
QRS ≥ 120 ms, broad notched R in I, aVL, V5, V6; deep S in V1-V2. T wave often opposite to QRS.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
幾乎總是異常。常伴隨結構性心臟病、高血壓、缺血性心肌病、主動脈瓣疾病。若合併胸痛且為新發生,臨床上以 STEMI 等級處理。
Almost always abnormal. Associated with structural heart disease, hypertension, ischemic cardiomyopathy, aortic valve disease. New LBBB with chest pain treated as STEMI.
WPW · DELTA WAVE Short PR · delta · wide QRS
預激 / Pre-excitation

沃夫-帕金森-懷特症候群 (WPW)

Wolff-Parkinson-White
波形特徵:PR 縮短(<120 ms)、QRS 起點有模糊的斜坡(delta 波 δ)、QRS 變寬。
Short PR (<120 ms), slurred upstroke at start of QRS (delta wave δ), wide QRS.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
存在額外傳導路徑(Kent 束),繞過房室結。可引起折返性心搏過速(AVRT);若合併 AFib 可下傳極快,十分危險。症狀明顯者可藉導管燒灼術根治。
Accessory pathway (bundle of Kent) bypasses AV node. Can cause AVRT; with AFib can conduct dangerously fast. Ablation curative when symptomatic.
§ 07 — 腔室肥大

厚重的心壁、擴張的心房

Chamber Enlargement

心電圖對腔室肥大僅屬粗篩 — 心臟超音波才是黃金標準。然而某些電壓與形態標準仍可強烈提示。

ECG is a rough screen for chamber enlargement — echocardiography is the gold standard. Still, certain voltage and shape criteria strongly suggest it.

LVH · PRECORDIAL Sokolow-Lyon criterion
肥大 / Enlargement

左心室肥大 (LVH)

Left Ventricular Hypertrophy
波形特徵 (Sokolow-Lyon 標準):V1 之 S 波 + V5 或 V6 之 R 波 ≥ 35 mm。Cornell 標準:aVL 之 R + V3 之 S > 28 mm(男)/ 20 mm(女)。常伴隨側壁導程 ST-T 段「負荷型」變化。
Sokolow-Lyon: S in V1 + R in V5 or V6 ≥ 35 mm. Cornell: R in aVL + S in V3 > 28 mm (men) / 20 mm (women).
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
通常源於長期高血壓或主動脈瓣疾病。需以心臟超音波確認。本身即為心血管事件的獨立危險因子。
Usually reflects long-standing hypertension or aortic valve disease. Confirm with echocardiogram. Independent cardiovascular risk factor.
RVH · V1 R/S > 1 in V1 · RAD
肥大 / Enlargement

右心室肥大 (RVH)

Right Ventricular Hypertrophy
波形特徵:V1 之 R 波高聳(R/S > 1)、V5-V6 深 S 波、右偏軸、右心房肥大(高尖 P)。
Tall R in V1 (R/S > 1), deep S in V5-V6, RAD, right atrial enlargement (peaked P).
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
肺動脈高壓、慢阻肺、肺動脈瓣狹窄、慢性肺栓塞。以心臟超音波確認。
Pulmonary hypertension, COPD, pulmonary stenosis, chronic PE. Confirm with echo.
ATRIAL ABNORMALITIES P wave morphology
肥大 / Enlargement

心房肥大 (LAE / RAE)

Atrial Enlargement
左心房肥大 (P mitrale):導程 II 出現寬(>120 ms)且切跡的 P 波;V1 呈雙相 P 波,末端深而負向。
LAE: Wide (>120 ms) notched P in lead II; biphasic P in V1 with deep negative terminal.
右心房肥大 (P pulmonale)
導程 II 出現高尖(>2.5 mm)的 P 波。見於肺動脈高壓、三尖瓣疾病、慢阻肺。
Tall (>2.5 mm) peaked P in lead II. Seen in pulmonary hypertension, tricuspid disease, COPD.
§ 08 — 缺血與梗塞

冠狀動脈的訊號

Ischemia & Infarction — The Coronary Story

心電圖判讀的核心任務之一。缺血、損傷、梗塞會依序在 ST 段、T 波、Q 波留下痕跡,從數小時演變到數月。

The single most important reason to read an ECG carefully. Ischemia, injury, infarction leave a trail in ST, T, and Q waves — evolving over hours to months.

ST ELEVATION STEMI pattern
急性梗塞 / STEMI

ST 段抬高

ST-Segment Elevation
波形特徵:連續兩個肢體導程 ST 段抬高 ≥ 1 mm,或連續兩個胸前導程抬高 ≥ 2 mm(V2-V3 閾值依性別、年齡而異)。多呈凸起的「墓碑形」。
ST elevated ≥ 1 mm in two contiguous limb leads, or ≥ 2 mm in two contiguous precordial leads. Often convex ("tombstone") shape.
臨床意義 — 急症 / EMERGENCY
急性貫壁性心肌損傷(STEMI)。導程分佈可提示罪犯血管:II/III/aVF = 下壁(RCA);V1-V4 = 前壁(LAD);I/aVL/V5/V6 = 側壁(LCx)。分秒必爭:目標 door-to-balloon < 90 分鐘。
Acute transmural myocardial injury. Lead groups tell you which artery: II/III/aVF = inferior (RCA); V1-V4 = anterior (LAD); I/aVL/V5/V6 = lateral (LCx). Door-to-balloon < 90 min.
ST DEPRESSION Ischemia pattern
缺血 / Ischemia

ST 段壓低

ST-Segment Depression
波形特徵:ST 段於 J 點後 80 ms 處低於基線 ≥ 0.5 mm。形態重要:水平型或下斜型比上斜型更具診斷意義。
ST depressed ≥ 0.5 mm, measured 80 ms after J-point. Horizontal or downsloping is more specific than upsloping.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
心內膜下缺血。運動負荷測試常見的陽性表現。若伴隨胸痛且為新發生 = NSTEMI / 不穩定型心絞痛。亦可能是他處 STEMI 的對側性變化。
Subendocardial ischemia. Common positive finding on treadmill stress test. New STD with chest pain = NSTEMI / unstable angina. May be reciprocal to distant STEMI.
T WAVE INVERSION Symmetric · deep
再極化異常 / Repolarization

T 波倒置

T Wave Inversion
波形特徵:T 波在本應向上的導程變為向下。對稱型、深(> 5 mm)、雙相型或「Wellens 型」較令人擔憂。
T waves pointing down where they should be upright. Symmetric, deep (>5 mm), biphasic, or Wellens-like are more concerning.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
可能反映缺血、陳舊性梗塞、LVH 負荷、肺栓塞、腦血管事件、心肌病變,或某些導程(III、aVR、V1)的正常變異。Wellens 症候群(V2-V3 對稱深倒 T)= 高度警訊的 LAD 嚴重狹窄。
May reflect ischemia, prior MI, LVH strain, PE, CVA, cardiomyopathy, or normal variant in some leads. Wellens syndrome = critical LAD lesion.
PATHOLOGIC Q WAVES > 40 ms · > 25% R
梗塞疤痕 / Infarction scar

病理性 Q 波

Pathologic Q Waves
波形特徵:Q 波寬 > 40 ms、或深 > 2 mm、或超過同導程 R 波高度的 25%,且出現於連續兩個導程。
Q wave > 40 ms wide OR > 2 mm deep OR > 25% of R amplitude. In two contiguous leads.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
陳舊性心肌梗塞的證據 — 電氣上「沉默」的疤痕組織。即便無症狀,亦改變心血管風險分層。
Evidence of prior MI — electrically "silent" scar tissue. Even without symptoms, this changes cardiovascular risk stratification.
POOR R WAVE PROGRESSION R fails to grow V1→V6
梗塞暗示 / Infarction pattern

R 波進展不良 (PRWP)

Poor R Wave Progression
波形特徵:R 波振幅從 V1 到 V6 未能正常增長(正常於 V3 應 > 3 mm)。
R wave amplitude fails to grow normally from V1 to V6 (should be > 3 mm by V3).
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
可能提示陳舊性前壁梗塞、左心室肥大、LBBB、慢阻肺,或單純導程擺放不當。單獨不具診斷性,需與過往心電圖比較。
Possible prior anterior MI, LVH, LBBB, COPD, or simply lead misplacement. Not diagnostic alone — compare with prior ECGs.
§ 09 — 電解質與代謝

血液化學的心電印記

Electrolyte & Metabolic Effects

心電圖對鉀、鈣與某些藥物敏感。異常的嚴重度,常在波形上有可預測的演進。

The ECG is sensitive to potassium, calcium, and some drug effects. Patterns evolve predictably with severity.

HYPERKALEMIA PROGRESSION K+ rising left→right
高血鉀 / Hyper-K

高血鉀

Hyperkalemia
演進順序:高尖對稱 T 波(帳篷型)→ P 波平坦 → QRS 變寬 → 融合為「正弦波」→ 心跳停止。
Evolution: Tall peaked symmetric T waves (tent-shaped) → flattening of P → widening of QRS → merger into sine wave → cardiac arrest.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
> ~6.5 mEq/L 可致命。原因:腎衰竭、ACE-I/ARB/保鉀利尿劑、橫紋肌溶解、大量輸血。緊急處置:葡萄糖酸鈣、胰島素+葡萄糖、碳酸氫鈉。
Life-threatening above ~6.5 mEq/L. Causes: renal failure, ACE-I/ARB/spironolactone, rhabdomyolysis, massive transfusion. Emergent: Ca gluconate, insulin+glucose, bicarbonate.
HYPOKALEMIA ST↓ · flat T · U wave
低血鉀 / Hypo-K

低血鉀

Hypokalemia
波形特徵:T 波平坦、U 波明顯、ST 段下移、表面上 QT 看似延長(實為 QU 融合)。
Flattened T waves, prominent U waves, ST depression, long "apparent" QT (actually QU fusion).
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
原因:利尿劑、嘔吐/腹瀉、高醛固酮、鹼中毒。易誘發心律不整,併用毛地黃時尤甚。
Causes: diuretics, vomiting/diarrhea, hyperaldosteronism, alkalosis. Predisposes to arrhythmia, especially with digoxin.
CALCIUM QT ↔ Ca²⁺ inversely
鈣異常 / Calcium

高血鈣與低血鈣

Hyper- and Hypocalcemia
高血鈣:QT 縮短(ST 段幾乎消失),極高時可見類 Osborn 波。
Hypercalcemia: Short QT (short ST segment), possible Osborn-like waves at very high levels.
低血鈣 / Hypocalcemia
QT 延長(主要為 ST 段拉長,非 T 波加寬)。易誘發尖端扭轉。
Prolonged QT (via long ST segment, not wide T). Predisposes to torsades.
DIGOXIN EFFECT Sagging ST · Salvador Dalí
藥物效應 / Drug effect

毛地黃效應

Digoxin Effect
波形特徵:ST 段呈「勺狀」下移,形似達利翹鬍子;QT 縮短;T 波平坦或倒置。
"Scooped" or sagging ST depression with Salvador-Dalí-moustache shape, shortened QT, flattened/inverted T waves.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
治療劑量下的預期變化,不等於中毒。毛地黃中毒另有特徵 — 雙向性 VT、伴阻滯之心房性心搏過速、高度房室阻滯。
Expected finding on therapeutic digoxin, not toxicity. Digoxin toxicity is different — bidirectional VT, PAT with block, high-degree AV block.
§ 10 — 特殊症候群

一眼認出的電氣指紋

Special Syndromes — Distinctive Patterns

某些心電圖形態本身即為診斷。快速辨識,往往就是救命的關鍵。

A handful of ECG appearances are themselves the diagnosis. Recognising them quickly can save lives.

BRUGADA · V1-V2 Type 1 pattern
症候群 / Syndrome

布魯格達症候群

Brugada Syndrome
第一型:V1-V2 出現凸起的 ST 抬高 ≥ 2 mm,後接倒置 T 波,形似鯊魚鰭。
Type 1: Coved ST elevation ≥ 2 mm in V1-V2 followed by inverted T wave, resembling a shark fin.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
遺傳性鈉離子通道疾病。有心室顫動致猝死風險,睡眠或發燒時尤其危險。可能需置入心律去顫器(ICD)。
Inherited sodium-channel disorder. Risk of sudden cardiac death from VF, especially during sleep or fever. May need ICD.
EARLY REPOLARIZATION Benign STE
變異型 / Variant

早期再極化

Early Repolarization
波形特徵:ST 段凹面向上抬高,常伴 J 點小切跡,以 V2-V5 最明顯;T 波高聳向上。
Concave-up ST elevation, often with J-point notch or slur, most visible in V2-V5. Tall upright T waves.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
年輕健壯男性常見的良性變異。然而位於下壁/側壁導程的某些早期再極化型態,與輕度增加的心律不整風險有關。
Common benign variant in young fit males. However, certain inferior/lateral patterns linked to small increase in arrhythmia risk.
PERICARDITIS Concave STE · PR↓ · all leads
發炎 / Inflammation

急性心包炎

Acute Pericarditis
波形特徵:多數導程呈凹面向上的 ST 抬高(非單一冠脈分佈區),PR 段下移(以 II 最明顯,aVR 反之)。經典分四期演進。
Diffuse concave ST elevation across most leads, PR-segment depression (especially in II, reciprocal PR elevation in aVR). Evolves through 4 stages.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
心包發炎 — 病毒性、自體免疫、梗塞後(Dressler)、尿毒性等。需與 STEMI 區分(局灶冠脈分佈、凸起抬高、對側反向變化)。
Inflammation of pericardium — viral, autoimmune, post-MI (Dressler), uremic. Distinguish from STEMI (focal territory, convex STE, reciprocal changes).
S1Q3T3 · PE PATTERN PE suggestion
肺栓塞暗示 / PE

S1Q3T3 (肺栓塞暗示)

S1Q3T3 Pattern (Pulmonary Embolism)
波形特徵:導程 I 深 S 波、導程 III 出現 Q 波且 T 波倒置。常伴竇性心搏過速、新發 RBBB、右偏軸、V1-V4 T 波倒置。
Prominent S in lead I, Q wave and inverted T in lead III. May accompany sinus tachycardia, new RBBB, RAD, or TWI in V1-V4.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
肺栓塞導致右心急性負荷的經典(但敏感度不高)表現。單純竇性心搏過速才是 PE 最常見的心電圖變化。確診仍賴肺動脈 CT 血管攝影。
Classic (though insensitive) sign of acute right-heart strain from PE. Sinus tachycardia alone is the most common ECG finding in PE. CT pulmonary angiogram is diagnostic.
HYPOTHERMIA · OSBORN J-wave · < 32°C
低體溫 / Hypothermia

Osborn (J) 波

Osborn (J) Wave
波形特徵:J 點(QRS 與 ST 交界處)出現向上的額外凸起,形成 QRS 後的「駝峰」。振幅與核心體溫約成反比。
Positive deflection at J-point creating a "hump" after QRS. Amplitude roughly inversely proportional to core temperature.
臨床意義 / Clinical meaning
典型見於低體溫(< 32°C),亦可見於高血鈣、某些中樞神經疾病,或為正常變異。
Most classic in hypothermia (< 32°C) but also seen with hypercalcemia, certain CNS conditions, or as normal variant.

§11 閱讀報告 — 十一步系統化檢查法

按固定順序掃描，是避免遺漏的唯一方法。以下是檢閱年度健檢心電圖的建議流程。

A systematic sweep — always in the same order — prevents missing findings. Use this flow when reading your own annual health-check ECG.

STEP 01

病人資料與紀錄設定

Patient & recording setup

確認姓名、日期、紙速 25 mm/s、校準 10 mm/mV。錯的設定 → 錯的判讀。

Confirm name, date, paper speed (25 mm/s), and calibration (10 mm/mV). Wrong setup = wrong reading.

STEP 02

心率

Rate

計算 R-R 間的大格數：300 ÷ 大格數 = 每分鐘心率。正常 60–100。

Count large boxes between R peaks: 300 ÷ (large boxes) = bpm. Normal 60–100.

STEP 03

節律

Rhythm

節律是否規則？每個 QRS 前是否有 P？每個 P 後是否接 QRS？

Is the rhythm regular? Is there a P before every QRS? Is every P followed by a QRS?

STEP 04

心電軸

Axis

看導程 I 與 II。兩者 QRS 皆向上 = 正常心電軸。

Look at leads I and II. Both QRS complexes upright = normal axis.

STEP 05

P 波

P wave morphology

看導程 II 與 V1 的 P 波形態與寬度 — 排除心房肥大。

Check P wave shape and width in lead II and V1 — rules out atrial enlargement.

STEP 06

PR 間期

PR interval

正常 120–200 毫秒。延長 = 一度房室阻滯；縮短併 delta 波 = WPW。

Normal 120–200 ms. Prolonged = 1° AVB. Short with delta wave = WPW.

STEP 07

QRS 寬度

QRS width

< 120 毫秒為正常。加寬 = 束支阻滯、高血鉀、或源於心室的節律。

< 120 ms normal. Wide = bundle branch block, hyperkalemia, or ventricular origin.

STEP 08

QRS 振幅

QRS amplitude

V1/V5 高電壓 → LVH；V1 高 R → RVH;全導程電壓低 → 心包積液或慢阻肺。

High voltage in V1/V5 → LVH. Tall R in V1 → RVH. Low voltage everywhere → pericardial effusion or COPD.

STEP 09

ST 段

ST segment

應位於基線。抬高 = STEMI / 心包炎 / 早期再極化 / Brugada;壓低 = 缺血 / 毛地黃 / 低血鉀。

Should sit on baseline. Elevation = STEMI / pericarditis / early repol / Brugada. Depression = ischemia / digoxin / hypokalemia.

STEP 10

T 波、U 波、QT

T & U waves, QT

檢查 T 波方向與深度;留意 U 波(低血鉀);計算 QTc。男 > 450 ms、女 > 460 ms = 延長。

Check T wave direction and depth. Note U waves (hypokalemia). Calculate QTc; > 450 ms (men) / > 460 ms (women) = prolonged.

STEP 11

對照過往、做出結論

Compare with prior & decide

與去年 ECG 對照。新出現的變化遠比慢性變化重要。這一步等同於 SOP 中的「acceptance criteria 驗收標準」 — 沒有對照就沒有判讀。

Compare with last year's ECG. New findings always matter more than chronic ones. This step is the ECG equivalent of SOP acceptance criteria — no comparison, no valid read.

關鍵原則 Key Principle

系統化的檢查順序,是對抗「模式偏誤」的唯一武器。看到明顯的 STEMI 就跳過 QTc 測量 — 正是漏診的起點。

Systematic order is your only defense against pattern bias. Skipping QTc because you "see" an obvious STEMI is how diagnoses get missed.

核心要點 Key Takeaways

Four things to remember after closing this document.

01

正常是判讀的起點

Know normal first

P-QRS-T 每一段都對應一個電氣事件。熟悉正常波形,異常自然顯眼。不先建立基準,任何「異常」都只是焦慮。

Every segment of P-QRS-T maps to one electrical event. Anomalies are only visible against a known baseline.

02

系統化順序勝過直覺

Systematic beats intuitive

11 步固定順序,不是過度工程,而是對抗確認偏誤的 SOP。專家也會跳步 — 然後就漏掉了 QTc 延長。

The 11-step order isn't over-engineering; it's an SOP against confirmation bias. Experts skip steps too — then miss the long QTc.

03

形態識別是核心技能

Pattern recognition is the core skill

STEMI 的墓碑、高血鉀的帳篷 T、WPW 的 delta、S1Q3T3 的肺栓塞指紋 — 這些形態本身就是診斷,認出它們能救命。

STEMI tombstones, hyper-K tent-T, WPW delta, S1Q3T3 in PE — these shapes ARE the diagnosis. Recognition saves lives.

04

ECG 是篩檢、不是金標準

ECG screens, it doesn't confirm

LVH、RVH、陳舊梗塞、心律不整 — 心電圖給的是線索而非結論。確診靠心臟超音波、壓力測試、核磁共振、心導管。別把篩檢工具當終點。

LVH, RVH, prior MI, arrhythmia — ECG gives clues, not verdicts. Echo / stress / MRI / cath confirm. Don't treat a screen as a destination.

思考練習 Practice Questions

Three questions to lock in the concepts.

Q1 概念理解 · Concept
為什麼一度房室阻滯(1° AVB)多為良性,而二度 Mobitz II 型卻可能需要節律器?兩者在解剖位置上有何差異? +

解答重點:差異在於阻滯的解剖層次。

一度 AVB 的延遲發生在房室結本身(AV node),這是一個有自主神經調控的組織,常受迷走神經張力影響。年輕人、運動員、睡眠中皆可見,不代表傳導系統有結構性病變。

Mobitz II 則位於房室結下方的希氏-浦金氏系統(His-Purkinje),屬於結構性傳導異常 — 一旦脫落,無自主神經緩衝,可能快速惡化為完全性房室阻滯(3° AVB),甚至心跳停止。這就是為何 Mobitz II 常需預防性置入節律器。

The difference is anatomical level. 1° AVB = delay at the AV node (autonomically modulated, often benign). Mobitz II = block below the AV node in the His-Purkinje system (structural, unpredictable, can progress to complete block).

Q2 產業應用 · CDMO Application
§11 的 11 步判讀法與 GMP SOP 有哪些結構上的相似處?若要把「閱讀 ECG 報告」設計成一份 SOP,驗收標準(acceptance criteria)應放在哪一步? +

解答重點:兩者皆為固定順序、不可跳步、每步有明確通過/失敗判準的檢查流程。

結構對照:

  • Step 01(紙速/校準確認) ≈ SOP 的「設備校驗狀態檢查」
  • Step 02–10(各項參數測量) ≈ 批次紀錄的「critical parameter measurements」
  • Step 11(與過往對照) ≈ SOP 的「acceptance criteria vs. trending data」

驗收標準應放在 Step 11。原因:前十步是資料蒐集,最後一步才是「判讀 vs. 歷史基準」的決策節點 — 正如 GMP 中 OOT(out-of-trend)判斷必須對照歷史統計,否則單一數據點無法定義「異常」。

Both are fixed-sequence, no-skip checklists with pass/fail criteria per step. Acceptance criteria belong at Step 11 — without historical comparison, a single data point cannot define "abnormal," same as OOT analysis in GMP.

Q3 批判思考 · Critical Thinking
若體檢報告上只有「ST 段壓低」四個字,能否直接下「心肌缺血」的診斷?哪些資訊缺失會讓你退回「需進一步評估」? +

解答重點:不能。ST 壓低只是「觀察」,不等於「診斷」。

需補充的關鍵資訊:

  • 形態:水平型或下斜型比上斜型更具特異性;勺狀壓低可能只是毛地黃效應
  • 導程分佈:集中於冠脈供應區(如下壁 II/III/aVF)比散在分佈更可疑
  • 深度:< 0.5 mm 常為非特異性
  • 臨床情境:有無胸痛?運動時出現還是靜息時?有無冠心病家族史?
  • 對照:新發生還是慢性?
  • 其他鑑別診斷:低血鉀、LVH 負荷型、藥物效應(毛地黃)、束支阻滯的繼發性變化

這正是「ECG 是篩檢而非金標準」的體現 — 單一發現不能跳過臨床整合直接下診斷。

ST depression is an observation, not a diagnosis. Morphology, lead distribution, depth, clinical context, prior comparison, and differentials (hypo-K, LVH strain, digoxin, secondary to BBB) must all be considered before calling it ischemia.

Bottom Line

三句話總結

核心洞察 Core Insight

心電圖判讀是系統化的形態識別,不是猜謎。固定順序、熟知正常、對照過往 — 這三件事把 ECG 從「猜」變成「讀」。

ECG reading is systematic pattern recognition, not guesswork. Fixed order, known baseline, prior comparison — these three turn "guessing" into "reading."

立即應用 Immediate Application

下次拿到自己的年度健檢 ECG 報告時,用 §11 的 11 步流程親自跑一遍。不是取代醫師判讀,而是學會在診間問對問題。

Next annual health check: run your own ECG through the 11-step flow from §11. Not to replace the cardiologist, but to ask better questions in the consult.

需要補充 Caveats

ECG 永遠是第一關的篩檢工具,不是結構診斷的終點。腔室肥大要靠心臟超音波、缺血要靠壓力測試或心導管、心肌病變要靠心臟 MRI。把 ECG 的線索當終點,是判讀上最危險的錯誤。

ECG is always a first-line screen, not a structural verdict. Chambers need echo, ischemia needs stress/cath, cardiomyopathy needs cardiac MRI. Treating an ECG clue as final is the most dangerous reading error.
